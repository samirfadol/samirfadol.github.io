I am a political science research assistant specializing in international relations theory and U.S. foreign policy. My projects investigate the intersection of neorealism and liberal internationalism, focusing on their visions for the liberal international order.

As a researcher in American political science, I use process tracing and comparative case studies to analyze great power politics within the rules-based international order. I am currently developing a research agenda anchored in canonical paradigms and deploying quantitative methods in support of qualitative research, with great interest in hegemonic stability theory and democratic peace theory.

I received a bachelor's degree in international affairs, concentrating on diplomacy and international security, from Qatar University in 2016 and a master's degree in politics and international relations from Doha Institute for Graduate Studies in 2021. I am fluent in Arabic and English; learning French and German.
