I am a political science graduate specializing in international relations theory and U.S. foreign policy. My projects investigate the intersection of neorealism and liberal internationalism, focusing on their visions for the liberal international order.

Drawing on process tracing and comparative case studies, I analyze great power politics within the rules-based international order. I am currently developing a doctoral research agenda anchored in canonical International Relations paradigms—particularly hegemonic stability theory and democratic peace theory—while integrating quantitative methods to complement qualitative analysis. I am preparing to pursue doctoral studies in political science.

I earned a bachelor's degree in international affairs, concentrating on diplomacy and international security, from Qatar University and a master's degree in politics and international relations from Doha Institute for Graduate Studies.
